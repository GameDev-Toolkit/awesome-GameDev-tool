# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want contributing to **awesome-GameDev-tool** to be a good experience for everyone,
regardless of experience level, language, gender identity, disability, appearance,
race, religion or engine of choice.

## Expected behaviour

- **Be kind and specific.** "This link 404s, here is the new URL" beats "this list is broken".
- **Assume good faith.** Most PRs come from someone who loves a tool and wants to share it.
- **Critique the link, not the person.** Taste in tools is not a moral failing.
- **Respect licenses.** Do not ask for or submit pirated assets, cracks or leaked SDKs.
- **Keep it on topic.** Issues and PRs are about this list, not about winning an argument.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexualised attention of any kind
- Gatekeeping ("you aren't a real gamedev if…"), elitism about engines or languages
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Spam, affiliate-link farming, or repeatedly re-adding rejected entries
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, discussions and any space where
someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close or revert contributions, and may block accounts that keep
breaking these rules. Reports go to the maintainers via a private issue or the repository
owners' contact address. Reports are handled confidentially, and retaliation is itself a
violation.
